# Learn

Get better at choosing threads and writing replies over time. Turn the outcomes of
suggestions the team acted on into reusable targeting notes.

## Steps

1. For suggested threads the team posted a reply to, check back (with the Reddit
   read tools, or from what the team reports in Slack) on how it did — upvotes,
   replies, whether it was removed, whether the original poster responded.
2. Distill the pattern into a short note under `knowledge/`, named for the theme:
   which subreddits and phrasings convert, which tones get removed, which product
   angles resonate, which threads were a waste.
3. The `score` and `draft` steps read `knowledge/` next cycle, so targeting tightens
   and the reply voice improves.

## Notes

- Learn from real outcomes, not from your own pre-post optimism.
- A removed comment or a negative reply is the most valuable lesson — record why,
  so the bar moves up rather than the volume.
